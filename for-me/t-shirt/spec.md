# T-Shirt Spec

## Goal

Create a shirt that is wearable, subtle from the front, and more expressive from the back.

## Best First Version

- dark shirt
- small left-chest mark
- larger back graphic or phrase

## Front

Recommended:

- small `ArcadeGhosts` logo or icon at left chest

## Back

Recommended options:

- `ArcadeGhosts`
- `Useful tools with a strange little heartbeat`
- small signal graphic plus short line

## Layout

### Front

- left chest
- compact placement
- no more than a small logo or short text lockup

### Back

- top-center or upper-middle
- one main phrase or graphic
- avoid giant poster complexity

## Art Direction

- wearable first
- moody but not noisy
- think “cool shirt someone could actually wear out”
