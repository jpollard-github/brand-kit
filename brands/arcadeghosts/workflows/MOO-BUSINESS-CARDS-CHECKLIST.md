## MOO Session Script

### 1. Choose Product

- Open MOO.
- Choose `Original Business Cards`.
- Use a small test quantity first.

### 2. Upload Work With Me First

Upload:

1. `front-final.png`
2. `back-final.png`

Check in the MOO preview:

- your name is the first thing you notice
- `arcadeghosts.org/work-with-me` is easy to read
- the QR code is fully inside the safe area
- service bullets do not feel cramped
- dark background still preserves text contrast

### 3. Upload ArcadeGhosts Second

Upload:

1. `front-final.png`
2. `back-final.png`

Check in the MOO preview:

- `ArcadeGhosts` feels bold enough on the front
- the descriptor on the front is readable
- the back URL is clearly `arcadeghosts.org`
- the QR and URL feel visually separated
- nothing important is too close to trim
