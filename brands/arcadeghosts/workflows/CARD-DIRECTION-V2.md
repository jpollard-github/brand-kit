# ArcadeGhosts Card Direction V2

Reference: 2026-07-03 EDT

This document captures the next-round card direction after the first printed-card lessons.

Version 1 was not a failure.
It validated the brand direction and clarified what Version 2 should do better.

The first print lesson was not just design polish.
It was proofing.

The printed ArcadeGhosts cards looked branded and premium, but they were not comfortable to hand out because the text was too small and the cards tried to do too much.

Use [docs/PRINT-PROOFING.md](../../../docs/PRINT-PROOFING.md) before any new print order.

## Lessons Captured

The first printed cards confirmed:

- ArcadeGhosts branding works well in print
- black stock and neon branding feel premium
- the QR code approach works
- two separate card types was the correct decision

The failures were process-related:

- too much information
- text significantly too small
- cards tried to explain instead of invite
- physical proofing happened too late
- real-world readability was not validated before ordering

## Core Rule

Do not merge multiple jobs into one tiny card.

`brand-kit` should treat these as separate card types:

1. `work-with-me-card`
   consulting and business development
   clear offer
   professional trust
   leads to `/work-with-me`
2. `personal-card`
   social, creative, and community use
   leads to the home page or about page
   warmer and weirder
   no sales pressure

Future networking products may also include:

- `conference-card`
- `speaker-card`
- `event-badge`
- `nfc-card`
- `digital-wallet-card`

Do not merge multiple jobs into one design just because the format is small.

## Work With Me Card V2

Purpose:

- consulting outreach
- business development
- first-touch credibility
- remember Jason and reach the right page

Overall direction:

- professional
- confident
- minimal

### Front

Recommended content:

```text
ArcadeGhosts

Jason Pollard

Small Software &
Workflow Improvements

arcadeghosts.org
```

Optional only if it stays comfortably readable:

```text
Thoughtful software. Clear scope. Practical next steps.
```

Prefer omitting the tagline if it makes the card cramped.

### Back

Recommended content:

```text
Scan to learn more
```

Large QR code.

Optional supporting line:

```text
Small projects. Practical improvements.
```

Keep back text minimal.
No paragraphs.
No service lists.

### Remove / Avoid

Do not include:

- dense service lists
- long taglines
- tiny explanatory copy
- too many URLs
- multiple competing CTAs
- `Software Consultant` if the clearer phrase is already present
- any text that is only readable close-up
