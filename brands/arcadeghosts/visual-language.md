# ArcadeGhosts Visual Language

Reference: 2026-07-03 EDT

This document is the canonical ArcadeGhosts visual identity for future visual work in `brand-kit`.

It defines the shared creative philosophy that multiple asset types can inherit.
It should remain lightweight, durable, and independent of any single generator, renderer, or AI image model.

## Ownership

- `consulting-business` owns engineering philosophy, cross-repo workflow strategy, work-order generation, and standards.
- `brand-kit` owns creative philosophy, reusable visual language, reusable image-generation guidance, and reusable design guidance.
- ArcadeGhosts owns implementation details such as Ambient scene files, manifests, rendering, and review packets.

## Overall Philosophy

ArcadeGhosts should feel like one long-lived world rather than a series of disconnected visual experiments.

The work should feel:

- atmospheric without becoming noisy
- warm without becoming sugary
- nostalgic without becoming parody
- polished without becoming corporate
- cinematic without becoming fantasy concept art

The goal is recognizably ArcadeGhosts visual identity that can stretch across multiple surfaces while still feeling coherent.

## Design Goals

- Build a visual system that stays calm around text and interface content.
- Preserve enough negative space that imagery supports reading instead of competing with it.
- Keep outputs grounded, tactile, and human rather than glossy or generic.
- Make the brand feel unmistakable over time without needing logos or loud branding devices.

## Image Character

- cinematic
- realistic
- subtle
- high detail

- grounded rather than fantastical
- not concept art
- not stock-photo energy

## Avoid

- people as focal subjects
- readable text inside imagery
- logos
- copyrighted characters
- recognizable brands
- busy compositions
- harsh daylight
- bright noon daylight
- oversaturation
- loud neon
- clutter
- generic corporate stock-photo energy

## Composition Principles

- prefer landscape-friendly framing where the use case allows it
- preserve comfortable negative space
- avoid dominant centered subjects unless a specific asset truly needs one
- favor scenes that feel lived-in, observed, or half-remembered

## Likely Future Consumers

Future ArcadeGhosts visual work should inherit this language rather than redefining it. Likely consumers include:

- Ambient Scene Library
- photography guidance
- illustration guidance
- wallpapers
- social cards
- networking assets
- website imagery
- future branding work

Ambient is simply the first explicit consumer of this canon, not a separate visual identity.
