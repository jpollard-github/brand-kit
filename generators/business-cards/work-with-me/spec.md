## Design Notes

- keep the front clean and confident
- dark background
- amber and teal accents are okay, but use them sparingly
- subtle `ArcadeGhosts` logo mark if it helps, but do not let it overpower the services signal
- back should prioritize URL, email, and QR readability

## Ordering Notes

- order a small test batch first
- matte or soft-touch finish is likely the best fit
- keep the QR code on the back only
