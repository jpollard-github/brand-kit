# Mug Spec

## Goal

Create a mug that feels most aligned with the site quickly and with low risk.

## Best First Version

- black or dark mug if available
- logo on one side
- short phrase on the other side

## Recommended Copy Options

Pick one:

- `ArcadeGhosts`
- `Somewhere In The Pines`
- `Strange little experiments`
- `Useful tools with a strange little heartbeat`

## Layout

### Side A

- centered logo
- keep it medium-large
- do not crowd it with extra text

### Side B

- one short phrase
- centered or slightly elevated vertically

## Art Direction

- dark base
- off-white text
- amber or teal accent if supported cleanly
